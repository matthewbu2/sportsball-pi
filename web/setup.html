Name this ticker

Its address on your network. Each ticker in the house needs its own name, like den or basement.

What do you want to follow?

Every game of each sport you pick shows on the ticker. Sports out of season just stay quiet.

Your favorite teams

Favorites get their own section, a countdown to the next game, full celebrations when they score, and the TV can switch on before they play. Tap to add or remove. You can skip this.

Loading teams...

Sound

It plays through the TV speakers.

How do you watch games?

So a touchdown shows on the ticker when you see it on TV, not before.

Your login (optional)

For tinkering: a user name and password to log in to the ticker over SSH (ssh you@@HOST_ONLY@). It's an admin account, so pick a good password. It stays on the ticker; nobody else gets it. You can only make it now, during setup. Most people skip this.

All set!
